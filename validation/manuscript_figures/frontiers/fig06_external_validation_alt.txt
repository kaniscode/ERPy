Six-panel comparison of default ERPy detections and expert negative-N1 annotations. The vote-weighted matrix contains 2,929.0 units positive for both, 24,806.8 negative for both, 3,117.0 default-positive/N1-negative and 1,268.2 N1-positive/default-negative. Overall agreement is 86.35%, with a 95% participant-bootstrap interval of 81.47–89.86%. Default detection rates are 76.03% for unanimous N1-positive ratings, 59.11% for mixed ratings, 9.28% for unanimous N1-negative ratings, 36.67% for single-rater positives and 4.02% for single-rater negatives. Four actual public-data mean and SEM traces illustrate the four joint outcomes, with q values 0.00990, 0.844, 0.0158 and 0.195. The annotations and detector target different response definitions.
