Negative-N1 comparison on 32,048 records from 13 participants, using human annotation votes as the reference. Each record contributes one unit shared equally among its available raters. (A–C) Sensitivity, specificity and positive predictive value (PPV) for archived ER-detect and two stages of label-free ERPy N1 analysis. Both ERPy stages require the same fixed negative-peak gate and joint projection–energy evidence. Screening uses joint p ≤ 0.05; the primary detection uses Benjamini–Hochberg q ≤ 0.05 after failed gates receive p = 1 within the complete finite contact family. (D,E) Paired differences from the archive at the nominal screening and family-adjusted detection stages, respectively. Intervals use 2,000 whole-participant bootstrap resamples within site and condition on the recorded predictions. Filled markers indicate nominal 95% intervals excluding zero; open markers indicate inclusion of zero. The screening-stage comparison is diagnostic and carries no family-adjusted error-control claim. These development-cohort results do not establish equivalence or noninferiority; the contrast intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted.
