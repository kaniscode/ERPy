Figure S1. Fixed synthetic injection stress test of label-free N1 detection. (A) Primary detector with the 50-µV baseline-SD floor; (B) otherwise identical no-floor ablation. Both retain the negative-peak gate, joint projection–energy evidence and family adjustment. Each cell contains five independent eight-contact families with two injected targets each; entries are detected targets/10. All 48 phenotype, trial-count, amplitude and noise conditions are shown for each rule. These contact proportions are descriptive, without binomial error bars, because targets within a family are correlated. At 500 Hz, stationary Gaussian AR(1) noise has correlation 0.6 and 25% shared variance across contacts. Only early-negative injections represent synthetic N1; positive, late and residual-artifact injections test phenotype restrictions. The other six contacts per challenge family remained noise-only and produced no calls. These simulations characterize the focused rule under the specified generator, not clinical accuracy or complete-pipeline calibration.
