(A) Default-detector calls (15–300 ms; Benjamini–Hochberg q ≤ 0.05) versus negative-N1 annotation votes on 32,121 records from 13 participants. Each record contributes one unit shared equally among its available raters; cell values are vote-weighted record equivalents. (B) Detection rates by annotation pattern. Bars and the stated paired rate difference use 2,000 whole-participant bootstrap resamples within site; nominal 95% intervals are conditional on the saved predictions. The difference describes N1-label association, not annotator correctness. (C–F) Mean ± SEM of ten trials for each of four examples with two raters. Stimulation and recording contacts, N1 votes and saved default-detector q values are shown. Gray marks the omitted 0–15-ms artifact interval; pale green marks the tested 15–300-ms interval. Each example was selected nearest the median log10(q) within its unanimous N1-vote/detector-call stratum, before waveform inspection. These examples illustrate both agreements and both disagreements. Panel F does not pass correction across 72 contacts (joint p = 0.0488; q = 0.195). N1 annotations concern negative responses at 10–90 ms; N1-negative votes do not exclude positive or later evoked responses.
