# External evaluation against released ER-detect annotations

Protocol drafted on 7 September 2026 before computing ERPy predictions or accuracy summaries from ds004774. This is a documented prospective analysis plan within this project, not a formally registered study.

## Data and purpose

Use OpenNeuro ds004774 version 1.0.0 (metadata commit 582149a14a29c29f765d71ed61ba376047cd6686), its public recordings, event/channel status, and released rater annotation matrices. Include all recoverable good stimulation pairs and good ECOG recording contacts; do not select pairs by response magnitude, label class, or detection result. Report recovered coverage by subject/site and reasons for omitted records. The paper's released averaged-waveform derivative cannot supply the individual trials required by ERPy and will not be treated as trial data.

The primary purpose is to measure agreement of a fixed ERPy detector with independent early-negative-response annotations. These labels address a narrower physiological/temporal target than ERPy's standard either-polarity broad-window conjunction. Concordance with these labels does not establish generic absence/presence of all responses, clinical utility, or anatomical connectivity.

### Provenance amendment before UMCU scoring

After the initial Mayo pilot was scored, source-event comparison established that ds004774 MAYO01 overlaps the previously used ds003708 sub-01 demonstration: all 17 selected LTG1–LTG2 good-event onsets, pulse durations/types/current and status agree exactly. The full event tables differ in coverage (425 versus 495 rows), so they are not described as identical dataset releases. No thresholds, response windows, seeds or detector parameters changed in response to pilot performance. The primary external-participant summary therefore excludes MAYO01 and covers the other 13 participants (four Mayo, nine UMCU); the all-14 reanalysis and the overlapping participant are retained separately. This amendment was motivated by recovered provenance, follows Mayo pilot scoring, and precedes UMCU scoring. It is not portrayed as an original registered exclusion. The source comparison is retained in `development_overlap.json`.

## Fixed preparation and methods

- Use the released good-event/good-ECOG status and recorded reference. Do not add filtering or rereferencing. Recover epochs from −1 to +0.35 seconds in physical microvolts and subtract the trial median over −0.5 to −0.02 seconds, as in the released ER-detect configuration. State the difference from the normalization window described in the article. Additive trial constants cancel from ERPy's primary statistics, but matter for a negative-peak comparator.
- Evaluate the standard ERPy response window 15–300 ms, and a prespecified early-window configuration 10–90 ms with artifact exclusion through 9 ms. Both use an available baseline −1 to −0.1 seconds; the detector uses its latest sample-matched segment within that interval. Keep the standard minimum of eight clean trials. Do not lower it after inspecting sensitivity. Record unavailable outcomes explicitly.
- Use exact randomizations at the implementation's thresholds, otherwise 5,000 Monte Carlo draws; alpha .05; reproducible seeds derived from subject, stimulation pair and channel. Within each subject/acquisition/pair, adjust over all finite eligible non-stimulation-contact joint p-values, including contacts with missing labels. Do not adjust only over annotated contacts.
- Omit computationally expensive optional cross-validated canonical energy if it is unused in evaluation; it does not enter the two primary p-values. Save component p-values, q-values, effect sizes, trial counts, timing, and unavailable reasons.
- The primary trial-level evaluation assesses the detector on released data eligibility. It does not silently add a new response-derived artifact gate and does not claim to validate the complete ERPy QC pipeline. Any additional QC analysis must be identified separately with its exact rules.
- Compare with the actual archived ER-detect negative-peak calls released in each dataset's `erdetect_data.mat` and its recorded baseline-SD multiplier 3.4. These are published software outputs, not a new source-package execution. Verify reconstructed waveform means against these archived means on shared records before interpreting detector differences. No new threshold optimization or invented comparator result is permitted.

## Labels, exclusions, and aggregation

- Annotation matrix values: 1 = N1 present; 0 = no N1; 2 = positive P1 and therefore negative for the published N1 endpoint; −1 = stimulation contact; NaN = unannotated. Check these meanings against the paper's comparison code. Exclude −1/NaN from scored labels and keep counts. Preserve the rater identity. Do not invent a majority consensus when there is one rater or a tie.
- Provide per-rater results; use an equal-weight within-record mean of rater-specific correctness for a pooled summary so multiply annotated records do not receive extra weight merely from annotation availability. Also report strict consensus records and disagreement separately, with their denominators.
- Where electrode coordinates exist, exclude recording contacts whose minimum Euclidean distance to either stimulation contact is strictly below 12 mm, matching the paper's comparison code (not distance to the pair midpoint). In the current release coordinates exist for Mayo but are absent for UMCU; a spatially matched Mayo analysis and a transparently different UMCU analysis must be distinguished. Do not impute distances or claim exact published eligibility reproduction.
- Report sensitivity, specificity, balanced accuracy, predictive values, agreement, evaluability, confusion counts, and per-subject/site distributions. Available-case accuracy must be accompanied by coverage; a workflow policy that treats unavailable cases as no call is a separate result. Avoid hiding minimum-trial exclusions.
- Quantify uncertainty by resampling whole subjects, with site-stratified resampling when both sites are represented; shared channels, pairs and raters remain together. Bootstrap intervals describe this convenience cohort and are not a guarantee of population representativeness. Report interrater agreement only for records actually rated by multiple people.
- No parameter or threshold tuning is permitted for the reported fixed-setting comparison. Any later exploratory tuning needs a separately labeled development analysis and subject/site holdout; do not use it to replace the original evaluation. The standard designation refers to the broad response window; the shared-reference, no-additional-filter input and prestimulus interval follow this comparison's explicitly declared settings.

## Reproducibility and limits

Retain raw-object/range checksums, dataset metadata version, extraction settings, software environment, code commit, seeds, row-level predictions, label joins, exclusion tables and figure-generation code. Public repository artifacts may include public-data-derived summaries and recipes, subject to the dataset's stated license. The governed CNS/ACC–PAG worked example is a separate resource and must not be mixed into this public validation cohort.

Reporting must distinguish paper-reported ER-detect results, newly reproduced comparator results, and new ERPy results. No inference of superiority is planned from unpaired published headline metrics.

## Post-scoring coverage clarification

The following clarification was added after the completed predictions were reviewed; it does not represent a newly prespecified exclusion or a change to any detector setting. Executed source eligibility requires at least five released good source events, matching the released comparison's source rule. This precedes the separate ERPy minimum of eight retained trials. Review identified 5,901 additional otherwise eligible labeled records with no matching source events in the pinned recordings, plus 73 with fewer than five source events, among the 13 independent participants. No verified source mapping was recovered for the extra annotations. They are retained in an explicit annotation-to-source flow rather than assigned detector calls.

The original source- and site-eligible performance denominator remains 33,694, with 32,121 evaluable records (95.33%). A secondary coverage frame that adds the 5,901 otherwise eligible absent-source records contains 39,595 records, of which 81.12% are evaluable. This broader frame describes available input coverage, not measured accuracy on missing inputs. The full mutually exclusive hierarchy, overlapping original reasons, participant-level counts, and single-rater composition of the absent-source records are available in the frozen coverage audit. No primary performance number or prediction was changed in response to this audit.
