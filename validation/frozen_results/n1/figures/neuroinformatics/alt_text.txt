Four panels compare the fixed10–90ms ERPy detector, archived ER-detect, and a participant-held-out N1 hybrid classifier. Hybrid sensitivity67.4% closely matches archived67.6%, with specificity98.0% versus97.6% and PPV83.3% versus81.2%. General early ERPy has sensitivity77.4%, specificity89.6% and PPV52.7%. Precision–recall curves place the hybrid above morphology-only and general-ERPy scores over much of the range; uncertainty and post hoc development limits are stated in the caption.
