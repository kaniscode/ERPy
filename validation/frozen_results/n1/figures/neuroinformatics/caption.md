Figure 7. Participant-held-out development of the negative-N1 classifier. (a–c) Sensitivity, specificity and positive predictive value for fixed early-window ERPy, archived negative-peak ER-detect and the N1 hybrid classifier on the same 32,048 records from 13 participants. Points use the original record-weighted expert-vote reference; bars are 95% intervals from 2,000 within-site participant bootstrap samples, conditional on the fitted cross-validated predictions. The hybrid combines morphology with ERPy reproducibility, energy and RMS features. Each participant was excluded from model fitting, feature standardization and inner-fold threshold selection for their predictions. (d) Precision–recall curves sweep pooled held-out morphology/hybrid scores or the frozen ERPy q value. The hybrid operating point in a–c uses separately selected training-fold thresholds. Models were developed after the original benchmark was examined; this is development cross-validation, not untouched external validation. Archived ER-detect outputs were scored rather than rerun, and classifier decisions do not inherit the general detector’s FDR interpretation.
